Movement holds more information than the eye can see.

Impaired hand control can make everyday tasks difficult. Today, progress is often judged by watching a movement and describing it. That is valuable, but subtle details of muscle activity can go unnoticed.

Observation has limits

Small differences in how a movement is produced are hard to see and hard to compare from one session to the next.

Muscle activity is hidden

The signals behind an attempted movement are not visible from the outside, even when the hand barely moves.

Few objective references

Without recorded data, it is difficult to review how a session went or revisit it later.

A wearable band and software, working together.

The SynaptiX band places FMG sensors against the forearm. They pick up changes associated with muscle activity as the user attempts a hand movement.

Our software processes those signals and shows them as a live 3D hand, along with session information that can be reviewed afterwards.

SynaptiX is a research and development project. It is not a clinically validated medical device and does not diagnose or treat any condition.
